AlgorithmTypeRate Increase RuleRate Decrease RuleProbe MechanismNotes
AARF (Adaptive Auto Rate Fallback)Threshold-basedIncrease rate after N_success consecutive successes at current rate (typically N=10 for increase)Decrease rate after 2 consecutive failures at current rate (immediate response to failure)None - relies purely on success/failure countsSimple, widely deployed in legacy drivers. Fast fallback but slow climb. Oscillates in marginal conditions.
AARF-D (with Dual AARF)Threshold-basedIncrease after N_success successes. N_success increases after each failed probe (1, 2, 4, 8...)2 consecutive failures -> decrease one step. Reset N_success counter.Probes next rate after stable period. Failed probe doubles next probe threshold.More cautious than AARF. Less oscillation in fading channels.
MINSTRELStatistical samplingMaintains per-rate statistics: success_count, retry_count, throughput_estimate. Always uses highest estimated throughput rate.Continuous: if throughput estimate drops, rate switches automatically. No hard threshold.10% of transmissions are probes at random rates. Random sample keeps statistics fresh.Best for real-world performance. Used in Linux mac80211, hostapd. Adapts well to rapid channel changes. Default in modern Linux Wi-Fi.
MINSTREL-HT (for HT/VHT/HE)Statistical samplingSame as MINSTREL but works with A-MPDU aggregation. Optimises for aggregate throughput not per-frame rate.Tracks per-rate success at multiple aggregation levels (1,2,4,8,16... subframes)Multi-rate retry: tries rate A, falls back to rate B, then rate C within single A-MPDUHandles MCS 0-31 (HT) or 0-9 (VHT) or 0-11 (HE). NSS selection included. Linux kernel 3.18+.
RRAA (Robust Rate Adaptation)EWMA-basedUses Exponentially Weighted Moving Average of success rate. Increase when EWMA > high_threshold (0.85)Decrease when EWMA < low_threshold (0.35)Probes one rate higher when stable. Uses conservative thresholds.Better at distinguishing temporary fading from sustained bad channel. Less reactive than AARF.
Vendor proprietary (Cisco/Aruba)HybridEach vendor uses proprietary variation. Cisco CCX: client reports link quality metric. AP uses combined RSSI + link quality to predict optimal rate.Vendor-specific. May include RF signature matching, spatial diversity info.Vendor-specific probe scheduling.Not standardized. Black box. Generally performs similarly to MINSTREL in practice.
