About

I’m Manish. I work at the hard end of networking — the problems that survive three tiers of troubleshooting and end up needing a capture, a timestamp, and a very careful reading of the standard.

Most of what’s on this site started as something I built to answer one question faster, then kept because it turned out useful. If any of it saves you an afternoon, that’s the whole point.
